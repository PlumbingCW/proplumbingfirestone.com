Baking Soda for Cleaning Sink Drains: A Firestone, CO Homeowner Guide

Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Firestone, CO

A battery backup sump pump is a second pump in the same pit, powered by a battery, that takes over when the main pump or the power fails.

Can You Clean a Sewer Line With Vinegar in Firestone, CO?

Vinegar will not clear a main sewer line. It is a mild acid that can loosen a little film inside a sink drain. It cannot dissolve a root mass, a grease plug or a sag in the pipe.

Clogged Sewer Line Repair: A Firestone, CO Homeowner Guide

A clogged sewer line usually shows up in more than one place. Several fixtures drain slowly, a toilet gurgles when the washer runs, or water backs up in a basement floor drain. Stop using water and find your cleanout.

Fixing a Leaking Single-Handle Faucet in Firestone, CO

Most single-handle faucet leaks come from a worn cartridge, seals or O-rings inside the body. Turn off the water under the sink, take the handle apart, replace the worn part and reassemble.

High Water Bill in Firestone, CO: How to Run a Meter Test

Shut off every water-using fixture, then watch your meter. If the leak indicator spins or the numbers climb while nothing is running, water is leaking somewhere between the meter and your fixtures.

How Long Does It Take to Replace a Water Heater in Firestone, CO?

There is no single time that fits every house. A like-for-like tank swap in an easy spot is simpler than a tankless conversion with new venting, a new gas line, or a moved location.

How to Find and Shut Off the Main Water Valve in a Firestone, CO Home

Your main shut-off is usually where the water line enters the house. Check a basement or utility room wall facing the street, a garage, or a crawlspace. Turn a lever handle a quarter turn until it sits across the pipe.

How to Install a Toilet in Firestone, CO (And When Not To)

Yes, swapping a toilet is one of the easier plumbing jobs, if the floor flange is in good shape and the new bowl is the right fit.

How to Vent a Tankless Water Heater in Firestone, CO

A gas tankless water heater vents exhaust outdoors through a sealed or open pipe, depending on the model. Direct vent units pull air in from outside and push exhaust out. Atmospheric units use room air.

PEX Pipe in Residential Plumbing: What Firestone, CO Homeowners Should Know

PEX is flexible plastic pipe used for hot and cold water supply inside homes. It resists corrosion, bends around corners, and goes in with fewer joints than rigid pipe. It is not perfect.
