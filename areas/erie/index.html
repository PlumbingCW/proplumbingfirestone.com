Pipe leak or groundwater seepage

The two look alike on a basement floor, but the clues differ. This split matters because only one of them is a plumbing job.

Signs of a plumbing leak

  • The meter keeps moving with every tap closed.
  • Water appears near a pipe, fixture or water heater.
  • It continues during dry weather.
  • A sudden wet spot after a cold night.

Signs of seepage or drainage trouble

  • Water shows after heavy rain or snowmelt.
  • It appears along the wall and floor joint.
  • Soil near the foundation is saturated or sloping toward the house.
  • The meter does not move when taps are closed.
