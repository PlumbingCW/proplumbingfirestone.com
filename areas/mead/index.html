Town service or well and septic: what changes

Mead has both kinds of property, so this decision runs through every repair. The lists below are general trade knowledge, not a finding about your lot.

If you are on town water and sewer

  • A shutoff valve at the house and one at the meter.
  • Water supplied by a provider you can call.
  • Sewer line runs to a public main.
  • Repair is mostly inside the property line.

If your property is on a well or septic

  • Supply comes through a pump and pressure tank.
  • Waste goes to a tank and drain field.
  • A backup may be a blocked house line or a full tank.
  • Some work belongs to well or septic trades.

Three clues to tell which system serves your house

  1. Read the water bill.A bill from a provider means you are connected to a public supply. No bill points toward a well.
  2. Look for a tank and a pump.Pressure tanks and well controls are usually in a utility space or garage. If you find them, tell us.
  3. Look for a cover or cleanout in the yard.A septic tank has access lids, while a sewer cleanout is a small capped pipe. A picture helps when you call.
