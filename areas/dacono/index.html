How a stubborn clog gets handled

Slow drains in Dacono are usually tackled in this order. If the first step works, the rest never happen.

  1. Tell us the pattern.One fixture or several? Several fixtures point to the main line, which is the job for drain cleaning with a cable machine.
  2. Stop sending water down.Pausing sinks, showers and laundry keeps a partial blockage from turning into a backup.
  3. The plumber clears the line.A cable or water jet breaks up the blockage and restores flow. The cause gets explained afterward.
  4. A camera if it returns.Repeat clogs suggest a root, a sag or a break. A sewer camera inspection shows which, so you are not clearing the same blockage again.
