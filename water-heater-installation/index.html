Sizing the tank to the household

A tank that is too small runs out mid-shower, and one that is too large wastes energy keeping extra water hot. The right size depends on how many people use hot water and when they use it at the same time.

Count the heavy users. A big soaking tub, a rain-style shower head, a dishwasher and a laundry load running together all pull from the same tank. Tell the plumber about those habits, because the number of bedrooms alone is a poor guide.

Firestone has grown quickly, with the population rising from 1,908 in 2000 to 16,381 in 2020. Many newer houses were built with a heater sized for the original family, so a growing household may be the reason yours no longer keeps up.

Venting and gas lines when the new unit does not match the old

A like-for-like swap often reuses the existing vent and gas connection. A different model, a higher input rating or a new location can mean new vent pipe, a larger gas line, or a new combustion air path.

Venting is where shortcuts do the most harm. A badly pitched or undersized vent can let exhaust gas back into the house, and a gas line that cannot deliver enough fuel makes a unit short-cycle. If you ever smell gas near the unit, leave and call the gas utility or 911 first. Colorado's 2021 plumbing and fuel gas codes, which incorporate the IPC, set the rules for these connections, so a licensed plumber should make them.

Electric and heat pump units skip combustion venting, but they have their own needs. They need a dedicated circuit, and a heat pump unit also needs a condensate drain and space around it for airflow.

Parts that belong with a proper install

A complete install is more than the tank. These items are worth asking about by name.

Expansion tank.

Absorbs the extra volume of water as it heats, which protects the tank and fixtures from pressure spikes.

Drain pan and drain line.

Catches a slow leak early, especially where the heater sits above finished space.

Relief valve and discharge pipe.

The safety valve needs a properly routed pipe to a safe spot, not capped or kinked.

New shutoff valves and connectors.

Old isolation valves seize over time. Replacing them while the lines are open makes the next service easier.

Gas shutoff and sediment trap.

On gas units, a clean shutoff and a sediment trap protect the control valve.

Permits and inspection: what to ask the Firestone building division

We do not state which water heater jobs need a permit, because permit rules vary. Ask the Firestone building division at 9950 Park Avenue whether your specific job does, and tell them if you are changing fuel or moving the unit.

If a permit is required, the Town's guidelines call for the licensed plumber and the state licence number to be listed on it. The Town verifies that the licence is active, and inspectors may fail an inspection if they cannot verify the plumber. Colorado licenses plumbers through DORA, so you can look up any plumber yourself, including ours.

The code base is the 2021 International Plumbing Code as adopted in Colorado. If your property sits in the rural area outside town limits around Mead, the Weld County Building Department is the office to ask instead.

Heat pump and tankless options

A heat pump water heater is an option to discuss when you replace a tank, though it needs room, a drain and the right circuit. A tankless unit needs its own gas, venting and flow planning.

Neither is a default. Describe your house and your hot water habits, and we will say whether either fits, and what it would involve.

Water supply questions worth a minute

The Town buys treated water from the Central Weld County Water District and the St. Vrain Water Authority. The district reports an average total hardness of 33 mg/L, which it calls soft, so a new tank in a home it serves does not automatically need a softener. The other supplier reports no hardness figure, so ask which one serves your address before deciding.

If you are weighing a softener or filter alongside the heater, that page explains how to test first. For work beyond the heater, such as new lines for a relocated unit, see plumbing installation.
