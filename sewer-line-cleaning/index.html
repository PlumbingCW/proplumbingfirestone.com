If a toilet gurgles while the shower drains, or a basement floor drain begins to rise, the main sewer line is probably blocked. Sewer line cleaning in Firestone, CO means clearing the pipe between your house and the town sewer, and our 24/7 line is open for it. While you wait, stop all water use, keep people out of the wet area, and do not flush.

A main line blockage pushes water back uphill, so the lowest drain overflows first. In many homes that is a basement shower, a floor drain, or a ground-level tub.

We will ask which fixtures act up and where your cleanout is. Those two answers usually tell us if this is a main line job or a single branch.

Signs the main line is blocked

One of these alone can be a branch clog. Two or more together usually mean the main.

Several fixtures at once.

A sink, a tub, and a toilet all react when you run water in one place.

A floor drain that rises.

Basement and garage drains sit lowest, so they show a main line backup first.

Gurgling from a toilet.

Air pushed ahead of a blockage escapes through the nearest trap and makes noise.

Water in the tub during laundry.

The washer drains into the same line, and a stopped main sends that water to the tub.

A sewage smell indoors.

Odor with slow drains often means wastewater is sitting in the pipe instead of leaving.

What a main-line visit looks like

  1. We find the way in.Usually a cleanout beside the house or under it. If there is none, we use a toilet or roof vent.
  2. We clear the blockage.A heavy cable cuts roots and paper. Hard grease or heavy buildup may need a jet.
  3. We check the flow.Running water through the line and, when it fits, a camera tells us if the pipe is really open.
  4. We report what we saw.If the pipe is damaged, we say so with the video, and you decide what happens next.

Finding your cleanout

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a plumber straight access to the main. Look near the foundation, in the basement near the water heater or floor drain, in a crawl space, or in a small box in the yard. If you are unsure, send a photo description over the phone and we will talk through it.

Open it only when the line is quiet. A capped pipe can hold back standing sewage, and we would rather open it with the right gear on.

Why backed-up sewage needs prompt attention

Wastewater carries bacteria, and it soaks into carpet, drywall bottoms, and cabinet bases quickly. A small spill that is cleaned the same day is a very different job from one left overnight. Stopping the water and calling right away protects your floors as much as it protects your plumbing.

Roots, wipes, and older laterals

Roots are the most common reason a sewer main blocks in an older neighborhood. They enter at joints, thicken, and catch paper until the line closes. Firestone's original townsite, platted in 1907, has older pipe and mature trees, so a main that blocks there deserves a camera look after the cleaning, not just a cable pass.

Newer homes built after 2000 more often see wipes marked flushable, grease, or a clump of debris. Whichever age your home is, tell us on the phone. We dispatch to ZIP codes 80504 and 80520, and the age of the house shapes the tool we bring.

If your property is on septic rather than town sewer, as some rural parcels around Mead are, cleaning the main is not the right job. Tell us at the start so the plumber brings the right plan. For rural parcels the Weld County Building Department publishes the adopted codes, so ask it which standard applies to your system.

Before the plumber arrives

  • Note when it started.After a heavy laundry day, during a storm, or slowly over weeks all point to different causes.
  • Find the nearest affected drain.The lowest fixture that is rising tells us where the blockage sits relative to the house.
  • Clear a path to the cleanout.Move boxes, stored items, and clutter so the plumber can reach it.
  • Turn off the washer and dishwasher.They push a steady stream into the line.
  • Keep the phone close.We may need to ask a question once the plumber is on the way.
