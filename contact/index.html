What to have ready when you call

You do not need to prepare much. A little information helps the dispatcher choose the right plumber for the job.

  • The street address and nearest cross street.This tells the dispatcher which town or ZIP code you are in, such as 80504 or 80520.
  • What you see, hear or smell.Say whether water is flowing, whether a drain is gurgling or whether a smell is coming from the heater. Say it first if there is gas.
  • Where the water is coming from.A ceiling, a wall, a fixture, a floor drain or a heater. Even a guess helps.
  • Whether you have shut off the water.If you could not find or turn the main valve, tell the dispatcher so the plumber can plan for it.
  • The age and type of the fixture, if you know.Tank or tankless heater, a toilet that has been replaced recently, an older sink. Do not worry if you do not know.

What happens after you call

  1. The dispatcher listens and asks questions.The questions are short and focused on safety first, such as gas and electrical risk.
  2. The job goes to a licensed plumber.We match it to a plumber who handles that kind of work. Same-day appointments are offered when available.
  3. You hear the price before work starts.The plumber explains the problem and the work, then gives you a clear price before work starts.
